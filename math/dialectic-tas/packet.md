proposition: "Packets with register=judgment or actor=human require body sections ## Thesis, ## Antithesis, ## Synthesis. The structural form of dialectics is mandatory for human judgments because human judgment without explicit consideration of counter-arguments slips into self-deception. The dialectic tradition shows that synthesis is reachable only through thesis and antithesis; collapsing this structure turns judgment into declaration. In software engineering, judgment about trust, security, or reversibility must contain an explicit antithesis — the strongest counter-argument against the decision taken. Without it, the proposition is a slogan and the reviewer cannot distinguish considered decision from impulsive one."
