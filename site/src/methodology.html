Methodology

The format of specifications and evidence is version 6; the tool is mathc 5 — the product version did not change. Math Coding manages claims about a result and records of observations. Its unit of work is a claim with a subject, boundaries, a falsifier and explicit acceptance conditions. It does not replace requirements, ADRs, tests or the development process: it links them and shows what is confirmed, stale or still unknown.

The complete standalone canonical methodology (Russian). This page is an overview, not a second source of normative rules.

From a claim to a verdict

  1. The goal records the human-provided aim, scope and owner.
  2. A claim names its subject, falsifier, register and evidence requirements. A decision source can be linked by digest.
  3. A verifier produces an observation: pass, fail, inconclusive, infrastructure-error or not-run.
  4. An evidence record binds that observation to the subject, definition, verifier, environment, producer and time; the fingerprint of the tool is written separately from the environment.
  5. States and verdicts are computed from applicable records. A successful process alone does not establish holds.

The control checks the check

The control proves that a check can reject one concrete violation, and for that the check has to run in the copy before the control is applied. The protocol: the same check must first pass on an untouched copy of the subject; only then is the control applied and the check repeated, and it must return an ordinary fail. A copy that cannot run the check at all — missing dependencies, no toolchain — proves nothing by failing: the observation becomes inconclusive with the reason copy-failed, and a machine holds is then impossible.

Only the combination — the subject changed and the check rejected the change — proves that the control reaches the subject, and it proves sensitivity to that particular violation only. A control that changes nothing, changes unrelated files or fails to break the check yields inconclusive, not refuted. The live tree is left untouched.

A check that needs the dependencies of the project declares the environment it runs in: the environment prefix prepares that environment before the check runs. Without the declaration the copy cannot run the check, and the reason copy-failed says so instead of a false proof.

Freshness and independence have limits

Evidence is current only while the materials, definition, verifier/control and environment fingerprints match and its validity window is open. The environment is the operating system and the pinned toolchain (the lock file); the build of the tool is written separately, next to the environment. The tool fingerprint is not part of that comparison: a new build does not make an observation outdated, the map names the reason tool-changed, and the result is not revoked — the check was the same and the subject was the same. The actual working contents, including untracked files and modes, matter more than HEAD alone. A changed source requires human review and a fresh run.

In format 5 the binary was inside the fingerprint of the environment, so rebuilding the tool made every piece of evidence everywhere stale at once: in one project 151 confirmations went stale although none of them had become false.

self means a self-declaration, fresh-context means a separate verifier execution with a recorded producer, and external means an external system or human review. These levels describe provenance, not an independent author of the requirement or test. Trust is never raised by fallback.

Claim states

statemeaning
holdsevery required condition has fresh, sufficiently independent evidence; a machine verifier also has a proven subject control
refutedan applicable observation rejected the claim
inconclusiveobservations neither establish nor refute the claim; a weak or missing control belongs here
outdatedmaterials, definition, verifier, environment or source changed, or validity expired
never-checkedno applicable observation has been recorded
waivedan active human acceptance of an uncertain risk; neither a pass nor a cover for refutation

Verdict and coverage are different

verdictcondition
greena non-empty set of claims, all holds
redat least one claim is refuted; refutation dominates
unknownno refutation, but not every claim holds
vacantthere are no claims

Green means neither full coverage nor correctness of the whole program. Goal-scope paths outside every claim are reported separately as uncovered. Established claims, a fully inspected scope and human acceptance are three different outcomes.

Composing requirements

All: every branch is required

Any fail refutes All and stops execution. After inconclusive, infrastructure-error or not-run, the remaining branches still execute so that a later refutation is not lost.

Any: one alternative is enough

The first pass stops execution. Any is refuted only when every alternative is refuted; otherwise an unconfirmed result remains uncertain.

Registers and human decisions

fact, hypothesis and unknown share the same evidence rules: the register describes the kind of claim and does not lower the standard. judgment is established only by an external human review. A review record has an author and an outcome; a source review has an author, a reason and the previous digest.

A human decision is bound to the decision, not to the bytes of the document that carries it: a document that keeps growing (a plan, a wave journal) does not make the decision stale. The decision stays applicable while the definition of the claim, the review condition, the term and the required independence are unchanged. A decision that changed in substance is revised explicitly — mathc review-decision.

A waiver is bounded by its gap, issuer, reason, expiry and recovery plan. The schema also carries compensating controls and review triggers; the standard CLI leaves those two lists empty. Reviews, waivers, source reviews and authorizations are decision journals, not authentication.

How the state reaches the reader

The kernel computes the states; the interfaces only show them. The plugin for Paseo and the local browser page are two hosts over one interface, and the only source of the verdict and of a state is the output of mathc map --json. Freshness, independence and the reach of the control are never recomputed outside the kernel: an observation pass does not become holds, a waiver does not become green and not-run does not become refuted.

On this site the same rule holds: the grid on the home page is rendered from the live map, and it shows a red or an unknown map exactly as honestly as a green one. The console, piece by piece.
