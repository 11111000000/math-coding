Methodology

math-coding v5 keeps one standing specification per project: a goal set by a human, and a set of claims. Each claim binds a requirement to a scope, a falsifier, a register and a control. The kernel does not write content into this model — it checks that the content is honest, and computes states that cannot be edited by hand.

Evidence is linked and fresh

An evidence record carries the digests of everything it depended on. If any of the four changes, the evidence stops being current — the claim becomes outdated, and no green verdict is possible until a new run.

Independence is explicit

Who produced the evidence is part of the evidence. Trust rises along the ladder and never rises by fallback: a claim that declares an independent check is not promoted because only a self-check is available.

The laws the kernel enforces

unknown ≠ pass

Not-run is not failure and not success. Unknown cannot be hidden by acceptance, standing, a missing record or an empty scope.

refutation dominates

A single refuted claim makes the whole map red. Confidence does not average out a falsified requirement.

freshness is multi-factor

Evidence expires when the claim definition, the verifier, the materials or the environment fingerprint changes, or when its validity window closes. The current environment must match the evidence, not merely exist.

no green without a fresh run

A machine holds requires a fresh pass, a control that reached the subject, and satisfied independence requirements. After review-source, a new run is mandatory.

history is never rewritten

A changed ADR yields source_changed: the claim goes outdated, the waiver is cleared, and the previous digest is kept. The historical decision itself is never edited.

judgment is human

The judgment register is installed only by an external human review. A machine verifier produces an observation; it neither installs nor refutes a judgment.

waivers are bounded

A waiver has a subject, an issuer, a reason, an expiry, compensating controls and a recovery plan. It is valid only inside [issued_at, expires_at) and never turns a failure into a pass.

Registers

registerwho can confirm it
facta machine verifier, re-run in a fresh context
hypothesisa machine verifier — until the falsifier fires
judgmentonly an external human review, recorded with author and reason

The kernel constrains what may be claimed. The human decides what to do with the verdict — and the decision itself is recorded.
