Methodology

Math Coding manages claims about a result and records of observations. Its unit of work is a claim with a subject, boundaries, a falsifier and explicit acceptance conditions. It does not replace requirements, ADRs, tests or the development process: it links them and shows what is confirmed, stale or still unknown.

The complete standalone canonical methodology (Russian). This page is an overview, not a second source of normative rules.

From a claim to a verdict

  1. The goal records the human-provided aim, scope and owner.
  2. A claim names its subject, falsifier, register and evidence requirements. A decision source can be linked by digest.
  3. A verifier produces an observation: pass, fail, inconclusive, infrastructure-error or not-run.
  4. An evidence record binds that observation to the subject, definition, verifier, environment, producer and time.
  5. States and verdicts are computed from applicable records. A successful process alone does not establish holds.

The control checks the check

After an initial pass, the control changes the subject in an isolated copy. The same verifier runs again and must return an ordinary fail. This proves sensitivity to that particular violation only. A control that changes nothing, changes unrelated files or fails to break the verifier yields inconclusive, not refuted. The live tree is left untouched.

Freshness and independence have limits

Evidence is current only while the materials, definition, verifier/control and environment fingerprints match and its validity window is open. The actual working contents, including untracked files and modes, matter more than HEAD alone. A changed source requires human review and a fresh run.

self means a self-declaration, fresh-context means a separate verifier execution with a recorded producer, and external means an external system or human review. These levels describe provenance, not an independent author of the requirement or test. Trust is never raised by fallback.

Claim states

statemeaning
holdsevery required condition has fresh, sufficiently independent evidence; a machine verifier also has a proven subject control
refutedan applicable observation rejected the claim
inconclusiveobservations neither establish nor refute the claim; a weak or missing control belongs here
outdatedmaterials, definition, verifier, environment or source changed, or validity expired
never-checkedno applicable observation has been recorded
not-checkableno suitable check exists in the current formulation; reserved in the domain and not produced by the current run
waivedan active human acceptance of an uncertain risk; neither a pass nor a cover for refutation

Verdict and coverage are different

verdictcondition
greena non-empty set of claims, all holds
redat least one claim is refuted; refutation dominates
unknownno refutation, but not every claim holds
vacantthere are no claims

Green means neither full coverage nor correctness of the whole program. Goal-scope paths outside every claim are reported separately as uncovered. Established claims, a fully inspected scope and human acceptance are three different outcomes.

Composing requirements

All: every branch is required

Any fail refutes All and stops execution. After inconclusive, infrastructure-error or not-run, the remaining branches still execute so that a later refutation is not lost.

Any: one alternative is enough

The first pass stops execution. Any is refuted only when every alternative is refuted; otherwise an unconfirmed result remains uncertain.

Registers and human decisions

fact, hypothesis and unknown share the same evidence rules: the register describes the kind of claim and does not lower the standard. judgment is established only by an external human review. A review record has an author and an outcome; a source review has an author, a reason and the previous digest.

A waiver is bounded by its gap, issuer, reason, expiry and recovery plan. The schema also carries compensating controls and review triggers; the standard CLI leaves those two lists empty. Reviews, waivers, source reviews and authorizations are decision journals, not authentication.
